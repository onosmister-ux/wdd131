As It Was - Website Plan

Site Name

As It Was

This name reflects how love and marriage used to be simple, real, and genuine in Nigeria compared to today.

Site Purpose

This website will share stories, videos, and insights about traditional African relationships and modern love, helping people understand the difference and value real love.

Scenarios

  • How was marriage done in Nigeria in the past?
  • Why is love today more complicated and less genuine?

Color Schema

Primary: #8B0000 – used for headings and header

Secondary: #F5DEB3 – used for background

Accent: #DAA520 – used for borders and highlights

Typography

Poppins – used for headings and body text.
