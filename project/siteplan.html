As It Was - Website Plan

Site Name

As It Was

This name reflects the idea of going back to how love, relationships, and marriage used to be in Africa, especially Nigeria, where love was more genuine and less complicated.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to tell stories, share videos, and educate people about traditional African relationships and how they compare to modern-day love. It will also inspire people to value real love, respect, and commitment.

Scenarios

  • How were marriages conducted in Nigeria in the old days?
  • Why does modern love feel less genuine compared to the past?

Color Schema

Primary Color: #8B0000 (Dark Red – used for headers and important sections)

Secondary Color: #F5DEB3 (Light Cream – used for background)

Accent Color: #DAA520 (Gold – used for highlights and borders)

Typography

Poppins: Used for headings and body text to give a clean and modern feel.
